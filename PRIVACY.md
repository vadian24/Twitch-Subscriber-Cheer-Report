# Privacy Policy — Twitch Subscriber & Cheer Report

**Effective date:** October 14, 2026

Twitch Subscriber & Cheer Report is a browser extension that helps users review subscription and cheer (Bits) activity from publicly accessible Twitch chat-log archives across selected dates.

## Information processed

To generate a report, the extension accesses publicly available archived chat-log information for the channel and date range selected by the user. Such logs may contain Twitch usernames, timestamps, subscription events, cheer activity, and related messages. **A public username can still be personal information.**

The extension uses this information to identify and organize subscription and cheer events for the user's report. The developer does not request names, email addresses, passwords, payment details, or Twitch account credentials through the extension.

## How information is used

Information obtained from the publicly accessible log source is used to create the requested subscriber and cheer report. Reports are intended for viewing and analysis by the user, rather than identifying people beyond the public account names appearing in the original logs.

## Collection, storage, and sharing

The extension is not designed to send report information, browsing history, or personal details to the extension developer. The developer does not operate a database of reports or user activity and does not sell or share data collected by the extension.

The extension accesses a third-party public log website to retrieve the selected archived logs. That website and the user's browser or network provider may process technical information under their own policies. Any report that the user chooses to copy, save, or share is under the user's control.

## Permissions and external sources

The extension needs access to the relevant public log source to fetch the archive entries requested by the user. It does not need access to private Twitch messages or the user's Twitch account.

## Data retention

The developer does not maintain a server-side archive of the user's reports. Information may be held temporarily in browser memory while a report is generated. If the user downloads, copies, or saves a report, that copy remains wherever the user chooses to store it.

## Children's privacy

This extension is not specifically directed to children and does not intentionally request personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's behavior changes. The latest version will be published in this repository.
